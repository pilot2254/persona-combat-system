overview:
- ambush on enemies: player initiates the battle, gaining the first turn advantage.
- enemy ambush on player: enemies gain the first turn putting the player at a disadvantage
- standard encounter: hhoever hits first gains the initial advantage without guaranteed turn precedence

each character shares identical menu options during their turn, simplifying navigation while maintaining strategic depth. Key battle commands:

- attack: executes a standard physical attack, which counts as having a "normal" element
- item: healing items, sp recovery items, and other typical jrpg consumables
- guard: reduces damage from incoming attacks but does not guarantee survival
- gun: characters have unique firearms with limited ammo, replenished only after leaving the dungeon
- summon persona: each character summons their unique Persona to perform elemental or skill-based attacks

- player can equip and switch between multiple personas during one battle
persona slots: limited in number, new slots open as the game progresses
- switching personas allows manipulation of attack types weaknesses. personas gain experience only when used in battle.
- each persona has defined strengths and weaknesses to different elements

- enemies show elemental weaknesses on an analysis screen.
- hitting an enemy with an attack they are weak against induces a “knockdown” state, temporarily incapacitating them.
- knockdowns enable players to enter a special state called “Hold Up Mode.”

- in hold up mode, players gain additional options instead of direct attack actions:
  - talk: Attempt to negotiate with enemies to recruit them as new Personas.
  - ask for money or items: Enemies may surrender valuables and flee.
  - recruitment success depends on the enemy’s level relative to the player.
  - if negotiation fails, the enemy might flee or attack again.
  - recruiting new Personas adds them permanently to the players collection
  - main characterss ability to collect multiple personas makes the system comparable to pokemon

- pressing the designated button initiates an All Out Attack, where all team members perform a powerful combined assault.. this move deals heavy damage to enemies but does not guarantee victory against resistant enemies
